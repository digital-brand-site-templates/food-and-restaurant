Dinner & Dessert

Dinner service

Dinner runs from six until the last table leaves, seven nights a week. The menu is short — six starters, seven mains, four desserts — and changes with the season; what is on it is made in this kitchen the same day, from vegetables that arrive from three farms within an hour's drive and fish from the morning market.

The set menu

Tuesday to Thursday, two courses for $24 or three for $29, chosen from the whole menu. Add a glass of the house pour for $5. Not available on public holidays or for tables of more than eight.

Dessert and after

  • Ice cream churned here in small batches — three scoops, $6
  • Chocolate fondant with salted caramel ice cream, $9
  • Cheesecake with berry compote, $8; affogato, $6
  • The bar stays open as long as there is someone at it

Good to know

Every dish is labelled for the eight common allergens and the kitchen can adapt most of them — tell us when you book. Children are welcome at every sitting; high chairs and a small plates menu are available. Private dining for up to twenty is in the back room, with a set menu chosen with the chef.

About Us

A neighbourhood restaurant with a short seasonal menu, bread from our own oven and a terrace for the summer. Breakfast from seven, lunch from eleven, dinner until the last table leaves; Sunday brunch from ten. Sixty seats inside, thirty out, and a back room for twenty.

Sunday brunch is back

27 march 2017

We cook from a short menu that changes with the season, and everything on it is made in this kitchen. Breakfast is served from seven, lunch from eleven, and dinner until the last table leaves. Our bread comes out of the oven every morning; the pasta is rolled the same afternoon it is served. The dining room seats sixty, the terrace another thirty when the weather allows. Book a table online or by phone; walk-ins are welcome when there is a free table. The kitchen closes at ten; the bar stays open as long as there is someone at it.

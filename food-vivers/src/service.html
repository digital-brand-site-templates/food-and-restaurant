About Us

A neighbourhood restaurant with a short seasonal menu, bread from our own oven and a terrace for the summer. Breakfast from seven, lunch from eleven, dinner until the last table leaves; Sunday brunch from ten. Sixty seats inside, thirty out, and a back room for twenty.

About Fritz

Fritz started as a dishwasher in his uncle's bistro in Lyon at sixteen and left it fourteen years later as head chef. Two years in Copenhagen taught him to cook with what the season gives rather than what the supplier sells, and that is the kitchen he built here in 2012: a short menu, three farms, one fish market, bread from the oven every morning.

He still writes the menu by hand on Monday mornings, still tastes every sauce before service, and still gets cross when the fondant comes out a minute late.

Signature dishes

  • Sourdough with cultured butter — on every table, every day
  • Rib-eye with peppercorn sauce and hand-cut fries
  • Wild mushroom risotto, finished with truffle in autumn
  • The chocolate fondant with salted caramel ice cream

Tasting evenings

One Thursday a month Fritz cooks a six-course menu for twenty guests in the back room, with a glass matched to each course. $75 a head; dates and the next menu are on the events page. Cooking classes — fresh pasta, bread, knife skills — run on Saturday mornings.

About Us

A neighbourhood restaurant with a short seasonal menu, bread from our own oven and a terrace for the summer. Breakfast from seven, lunch from eleven, dinner until the last table leaves; Sunday brunch from ten. Sixty seats inside, thirty out, and a back room for twenty.

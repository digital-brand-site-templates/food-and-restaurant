FoodVivers — Food and Restaurant HTML Template
===============================================

Version 2.0 — 2026
------------------

A rebuild on a modern stack, a menu page with an actual menu, four pages
the site needed but never had, a working search, a reservation email,
and a modernised design in the template's own colours: black, white and
the blue — with a heading on every section.


THE MENU, FOUR NEW PAGES AND AN EMAIL
- menu.html — the priced menu the page never had: Starters, Mains,
  Breakfast & lunch, Drinks & beverages, Desserts & ice cream, each
  line with a description and a price, above version 1's photo grid.
  The header search filters it (menu.html?q=…); the food tabs' tiles and
  the sidebar labels link there the same way.
- reservation.html — the booking form on its own page: date, time,
  guests, name, phone, email, notes; validates and confirms; "What
  happens next" in three steps.
- service-details.html — the page behind the four service icons:
  dinner service, the set menu, desserts, good to know, a booking card
  with hours, seats and the chef, and the other services.
- chef.html — a profile behind every chef's photo and name: the story,
  signature dishes, tasting evenings, and the rest of the kitchen.
- 404.html — a not-found page in the template's design with four ways
  back in.
- email-reservation.html — the confirmation a guest gets after booking:
  the logo bar and photograph, the booking card (date, time, guests,
  table, booked by), three "good to know" points, "Add to calendar" and
  "Change the booking". Table based and inline styled so it renders in
  Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail; {{name}}, {{date}}, {{time}},
  {{guests}}, {{table}} and {{email}} are the mailer's placeholders. A
  how-to-use note is at the top of the file.

THE DESIGN, MODERNISED
- Hero: a different headline on each slide, a line under it and two
  buttons (version 1 repeated one line and one button three times).
- Every section has a heading: Welcome, What we serve, Delicious food,
  In numbers, Our menu, What our guests say, Book a table, Meet the
  chefs — version 1 had none on most of them, and "DELICIOUS FOOD" and
  "OUR MENU" ran vertically down the left edge.
- Services: the four bare icons are cards with a line under each name.
- Hot deal of the day: the Lorem band is the Tuesday-to-Thursday set
  menu.
- Our menu tiles: a dish name and price under each photograph.
- Guests: the four client photos with a hover-only name become a
  "What our guests say" section with a line from each guest.
- Chefs: a role under each name.
- Counters in blue; a blue focus ring on the forms; a blue rule on top
  of the footer; the footer's "Twitter feed" (two Lorem tweets with no
  feed behind them) is the opening hours.

WORKING WHERE IT WAS DECORATIVE
- Search: both header boxes submit to menu.html?q=… and filter the
  dishes.
- 61 bare "#" links and 8 buttons that did nothing in version 1 — Read
  More, Book Now, the service names, the chefs, the sidebar labels and
  posts, the footer's social squares, the address — every one goes
  somewhere.
- Forms validate and confirm: reservation (home, about, menu and its own
  page), the comment form, the newsletter box. Every field has a label,
  a name, a type and autocomplete; "Select Meal" has real options; the
  Clear buttons are gone.
- The "Our menu" photographs open in a lightbox (version 1's CSS-only
  overlays, with their hidden full-size copies, are gone).
- Counters animate on scroll; the tabs, the three carousels and the
  collapsing menu are Bootstrap 5's own; Back to top — all without
  jQuery, counterUp or waypoints.

CONTENT
- 218 Lorem Ipsum passages are restaurant copy of the same length, so
  every block keeps its height.
- A title and meta description per page; alt text on every image.

UNDER THE HOOD
- Bootstrap 3.3.6 + jQuery 1.11 → Bootstrap 5.3, no jQuery, one
  dependency-free js/main.js.
- Font Awesome 4 (1.1 MB of eot / ttf / svg / otf) → Font Awesome 6 as
  three woff2 files (304 KB). Montserrat and Lato self-hosted as woff2
  (version 1 fetched both from Google — Lato ships as the exact files
  Google served). The aileron and antonio-exotic folders (1 MB, never
  referenced) are dropped. Nothing is loaded from a third party.
- lightbox.min.css (linked, never shipped — a 404 on every page) dropped.
- css/bs3-compat.css: the Bootstrap 3 behaviours this design relies on
  (the 10px root the type was sized against, fixed heading sizes, the
  1170 container, the navbar's block layout on tablets) restored on top
  of Bootstrap 5. Safe to trim.
- style.css section 8 holds the modernised look; delete it to get the
  version 1 look back on the same markup.
- The entry page is index.html (version 1: home.html, kept as a redirect).
- No inline styles or inline event handlers, no duplicate ids, lang="en",
  https links.

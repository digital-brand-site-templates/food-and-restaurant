About Us

The story behind Ci-treat — our kitchen, our people, and the ingredients we build every dish around.

20 Years Of Recognition

Ci-treat opened its doors in Hicksville two decades ago, and the same values still run the kitchen today.

Good food starts with good ingredients — everything else is technique.

What began as a single dining room on Duffy Street has grown into two locations, but the kitchen still runs the way it did on day one: fresh deliveries every morning, a menu that changes with the seasons, and recipes built around what's actually in season rather than what's easiest to stock.

Our chefs have stayed with us for years, not months, and it shows in the consistency of every plate that leaves the kitchen. Whether you're here for a quiet weekday lunch or a table of twelve on a Saturday night, the same care goes into the order either way.

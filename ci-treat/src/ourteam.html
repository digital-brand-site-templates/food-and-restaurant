Our Team

Meet the chefs behind Ci-treat's kitchen — some of them have been with us since the doors first opened.

Jim Brollian

Head Chef

Jim has run the kitchen since Ci-treat opened, and still tastes every sauce before it leaves the pass. Twenty years in, he still writes the specials board himself every morning.

Cooking is my life! — every dish gets the same attention, whether it's table one or table twenty.

Favourite Dish : Medium Rare Steak

Rested, never rushed — rosemary, cracked pepper, and cherry tomatoes on the side.

Max Benks

Head Chef

Max handles the grill station and most of the private-event menus, and has a habit of naming new dishes after whoever requested them first.

Low And Slow, Every Time. — he'll tell you the grill doesn't reward anyone in a hurry.

Favourite Dish : Pasta Carbonara

Eggs, pecorino, and guanciale — no cream, the way it's meant to be made.

Tom Whitfield

Pastry Chef

Tom runs desserts and pastry, and most of what's on the dessert menu today started as something he tested on the staff first.

Sugar Fixes Almost Everything. — usually said right before he hands someone a spoon.

Favourite Dish : Strawberry Ice Cream

Churned in-house, with strawberries from the same farm every summer.
